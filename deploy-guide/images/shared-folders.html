Where the files go on shared hosting

Your cPanel home folder (/home/<CPANEL_USER>). Only the "public" folder is reachable from the web.

/home/<CPANEL_USER>/
├── hotelms/               ← the app (upload + extract here)
│   ├── .env               ← your settings (secret!)
│   ├── app/ config/ vendor/ docs/ …
│   ├── storage/           ← uploads, logs (must be writable)
│   └── public/            ← the domain's document root points HERE
│       ├── app.html  assets/  sw.js  guide/
│       └── index.php  .htaccess  storage → ../storage/app/public
├── hotelms-backups/       ← BACKUP_PATH (outside the web)
└── public_html/           ← your main website (leave it alone)
cPanel → Domains → <YOUR_DOMAIN> → Manage
Document Root = /home/<CPANEL_USER>/hotelms/public
