**Title:** A new, cleaner look

**Text:**

HotelMS has a new look. Everything works the same way, in the same places.

- **Menu on the left.** Tap the button at the top left to make it small (icons only) or big again. On a phone, the menu slides in.
- **Your branch** is at the top of the menu. Tap it to change branch.
- **Search** is at the top. Tap it, or press Ctrl and K. Find guests, rooms, bookings and bills.
- **Light or dark screen:** tap the sun button at the top right.
- **Your picture** (top right, and at the bottom of the menu): My profile, Lock screen, Help, Sign out.
- **On a phone or tablet,** filters are behind the **Filters** button, so you see the rooms and lists first. The number on the button shows how many filters are on. Tap × on a filter to remove it.
- **A page you may not open** now says so, with a button back to the start page. Ask your manager if you need it.
- **Bigger buttons on touch screens,** easier to tap.

Questions? Help → How to use, or ask HotelMS support.
